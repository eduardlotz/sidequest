# Quest Design

---

# Primary Goal

Reduce _decision paralysis_

> "I own too many games and don't know what to play."

Every sidequest should make the player think:

> "Oh... XYZ would be perfect for this."

---

# What is a good sidequest

- Takes 20 - 60 minutes to complete
- Rewards meaningful progress
- Involves the current state of mind
- Inspiration instead of suggestion
- Not tied to one genre

Bad:

- Kill 10 enemies
- Jump off a mountain
- Craft an item

Good:

- Discover somewhere you've never been.
- Unlock something permanent.
- Become noticeably stronger.
- Finish something you've been avoiding.

Nobody thinks:

> "I want to play an Action RPG."

They think:

- I want to relax.
- I want exploration.
- I want to feel powerful.
- I want a challenge.
- I want something creative.

---

Every completed card should leave the save file in a better place.

Good outcomes:

- new weapon
- new ability
- new area
- new story chapter
- new base upgrade
- new achievement
- new companion
- new resource

## Optional Modifier

- Minimalist
  Use only basic equipment.
- Pacifist
  Avoid unnecessary combat.
- Explorer
  Do not fast travel.
- Survivor
  Heal only when absolutely necessary.
- Fashion
  Wear your favorite outfit.
- New Toys
  Use a weapon you've never tried.
- Hardcore
  Increase the difficulty.
- Roleplay
  Stay in character.
- Minimal HUD
  Disable as much of the interface as possible.
- Night Shift
  Complete your quest at night if possible.

---

Every card should satisfy one of five player motivations.

## Discover

See something new.

## Improve

Become stronger.

## Create

Leave the world different.

## Overcome

Beat something difficult.

## Experience

Enjoy the atmosphere.

If a card doesn't satisfy one of these motivations, reconsider whether it belongs in the deck.
